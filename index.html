Frequently Asked

COMMON QUESTIONS

Are all cricket bats pre-knocked before delivery? ▼
Yes! All SPORTIFY TitanStroke English Willow bats undergo a 10,000-cycle computer-controlled pneumatic knocking machine process and edge conditioning prior to dispatch. We recommend 20 minutes of light throwdown knocking with an old leather ball before full match play.
How long does express delivery take across India? ▼
Metro cities (Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata) receive delivery within 24 to 48 hours. Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities receive delivery within 3 to 4 business days. Real-time SMS and WhatsApp tracking links are dispatched immediately after packaging.
What is the return and replacement policy? ▼
We offer a no-questions-asked 30-day return policy for unused gear in original packaging. If you experience an unexpected handle defect or frame fracture within our 1-year match warranty, we provide an immediate 1-to-1 replacement.
Can we order customized club jerseys and team kit bags? ▼
Absolutely! Our Pro Academy division specializes in customized sublimation jerseys, personalized bats with engraved player names, and team kit bags with your club crest. Select 'Bulk Academy Orders' in the contact form or call 1800-SPORTIFY.
